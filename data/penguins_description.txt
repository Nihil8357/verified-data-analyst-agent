Measurements of 344 adult penguins from three species on three islands of the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica.
species: Adelie, Chinstrap or Gentoo
island: Biscoe, Dream or Torgersen
bill_length_mm, bill_depth_mm: size of the bill in millimetres
flipper_length_mm: flipper length in millimetres
body_mass_g: body mass in grams
sex: Male / Female (a few missing)
Source: Palmer Penguins dataset (Gorman, Williams & Fraser, 2014), via seaborn sample data.
