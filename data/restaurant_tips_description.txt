244 restaurant bills recorded by one waiter over a few months.
total_bill: bill amount in dollars
tip: tip amount in dollars
sex: sex of the person paying
smoker: whether the party included smokers
day: day of the week (Thur, Fri, Sat, Sun)
time: Lunch or Dinner
size: number of people in the party
Source: Bryant & Smith (1995), via seaborn sample data.
